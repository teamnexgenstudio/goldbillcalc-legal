Privacy Policy

App: Gold Bill Calculator (com.goldbillcalc.app) • Developer: Team NexGen Studio • Last Updated: November 26, 2026
Plain-English Summary: Gold Bill Calculator is an offline-first, 100% ad-free utility app. Your gold bills, shop comparisons, and chit records stay strictly on your phone. We do not require account login, we do not run ads, and we never sell your personal data.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All calculations, custom gold rates, saved bill history, shop comparisons, and gold chit records you enter are stored locally inside your device storage. We do not have access to this data, and it is never uploaded to our servers.

2. Manual Phone Backup & Restore

The app includes an optional Phone Backup feature that generates a backup file of your local records so you can save it to your device storage, Google Drive, or WhatsApp using Android's system share sheet. You are in full control of where that file is stored or shared.

3. In-App Purchases (Lifetime Pro Unlock)

Gold Bill Calculator offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (Lifetime Pro Unlock) processed securely through Google Play Billing and verified via RevenueCat:

  • Payment details (UPI, cards, or bank info) are handled solely by Google Play and are never seen or stored by us.
  • An anonymous, app-scoped identifier and purchase receipt status are processed over encrypted HTTPS solely to unlock and restore your Pro purchase across reinstalls.

4. No Ads & No Behavioral Tracking

This app contains zero third-party advertising networks, zero ad trackers, and does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

5. Deleting Your Data

Because Gold Bill Calculator V1.0 does not require user accounts or store your records on external servers, you can permanently delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Gold Bill Calculator is a general financial utility app and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce optional features such as cloud backup, we will update this Privacy Policy and notify users inside the app before any optional feature is enabled.
